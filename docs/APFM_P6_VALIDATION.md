# APFM P6: validation

Evidence and its limits for [`APFM_P6_MATCH_REALISM.md`](APFM_P6_MATCH_REALISM.md) and
[`APFM_P6_FINANCIAL_INTEGRITY.md`](APFM_P6_FINANCIAL_INTEGRITY.md). Branch `claude/p6-match-realism`, base `ea469f3a`; nothing pushed or merged.
Host: macOS, Apple Silicon, rustc 1.95.0. Labels: **RUN**, **READ**, **NOT RUN**, **BLOCKED**.

## 1. Verdict

**CONDITIONAL GO.**

Verified improvements, each with a test that fails without it: the engine prices numbers (a formation and a sending-off were worth exactly
nothing), a style's defence is applied (it never was), the dominant dials are rebalanced (best stack +0.23 to +0.27 → +0.08 to +0.11 points a
match), M12 seats every starter on his own slot (it did not, in all eight formations), the squad-floor waiver is bounded, a purchase meets the
senior limit, the manager's and delegate's books read agreed deals, and the imported world no longer starts structurally insolvent.

Why not GO: **no person has played the GUI** (the M12 slot fix changes the viewer); the numbers weights are **not M12-calibrated** and the two engines
still price formations differently; the squad model's formation score still does not predict results; possession and counter-press remain
the largest single dials; the importer rule is not mutation-checked; **the registry's contracted purchase is still exempt from the senior limit
(D7B3)**; the project reviewer agents were not run; M12's own tests were not run.
Why not NO-GO: no regression found. Invariants clean on every long run, no club in debt in the generated worlds, runtime and memory flat.

## 7. Limits of this evidence

1. **One seed** for the shipped and imported-world careers and for each cap of the importer comparison; three for the gate world.
2. **The engine changed**, so a comparison with P5 is across engines, same seeds, different trajectories; the studies reproduce P5's numbers only where P5 is quoted (I1 showed the I1 tree digit-identical to P5's).
3. **M12 was run only through the adapter in studies** (500 matches a shape; standard error ≈ 0.06): a 0.1 effect is under two standard errors. M12's own tests were not run.
4. The numbers weights are **not calibrated to M12**, which prices geometry; the grid fit improved the misfit only from 45.0 to 38.8.
5. The goal-rate gap between the engines (0.2, 1.4 standard errors) is **not demonstrated**; the dormant tier was not studied.
6. The dial effects are at equal players in one engine; on real squads they are within ±0.06 and several change sign.
7. **No GUI.** Nothing was launched, clicked or watched. The M12 slot fix is a viewer-visible change.
8. `engine::simulate` (the one-shot engine) was not changed and so differs from the live engine.
9. Disk was full once during the phase (the volume was at 97-99 % when it began); the session paused until space was freed externally. Nothing of the user's was deleted. Only the cloned `target/` of this worktree (Tauri's stale build output) was cleaned with `cargo clean -p tauri -p openfootmanager`.

## 9. Manual GUI checklist (for the user; none of it is claimed)

1. Re-import the FM24 world first (the world file changes; installed worlds are not adjusted).
2. New career on the imported world, a lower English club (`fm-c-440`, `fm-c-339`): the club starts without a cash warning and keeps its squad at the start.
3. In a 4-4-2, put a left back at LB and a right back at RB; play the match in the viewer: **each stands on his own flank**. Repeat in 4-2-3-1 and 4-1-4-1.
4. With a full senior squad of thirty, bid for a contracted player: the bid is refused for squad size. With an agreed deal waiting to register and one place left, a second bid is refused.
5. With an agreed signing waiting for the window and a budget with room for one wage, offer a free agent a contract: refused for the wage policy.
6. Quick-simulate a few matches, send a player off (red card) and see the match still plays; compare the goals against the viewer.
